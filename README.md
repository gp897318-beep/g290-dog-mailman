A single funny one-line review (119 characters) that a dog gives the mailman, written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-the-dog-reviews-the-mailman-87608f85) "The Dog Reviews the Mailman".

## The entry

> Six years he has stuffed envelopes through my slot and not one of them was addressed to me. One star, would bark again.

Ten candidates were drafted and compared against the judge's five criteria (on-subject dog review of the mailman first, then the dog's side of the visit, then a dog's voice, then a tight single line with a turn at the end, then a laugh that belongs to this dog and this mailman): a grudge about never getting a letter, a bribe ("I can be bought, but never cheaply"), suspicion that he is casing the house, the shoelace triumph, five stars for punctuality but zero for the sausage never dropped in the bowl, the grievance that he never stays for a sniff, the flinch after six years of barking, the gate toll of one biscuit, auditing his ankles, and the "would not recommend" review where the dog got told off. The letter grudge won: it is the only candidate built on the dog's own logic about the mail itself ("not one of them was addressed to me"), it keeps a dog's vocabulary (slot, bark, star rating), and its turn — "One star, would bark again" — is a review-format punchline that only this dog, reviewing this mailman, could write. Runner-up was the punctuality-vs-sausage line, which is just as much a dog's review but spends more words setting up the same six-year grievance and lands its turn less sharply.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (absolute path, as the competition requires) after checking the gate inside the program: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, a single line, not only whitespace, no leading or trailing space.
